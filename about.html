About Brethren Mission Ministry

Welcome to Brethren Mission Ministry. We are a Christian ministry dedicated to sharing the everlasting Gospel through Literature Evangelism, Public Evangelism, Bible Study, Prayer, and Christian Service. Our desire is to lead people to Jesus Christ and prepare hearts for His soon return.

Who We Are

Brethren Mission Ministry is an independent Christian ministry of Seventh-day Adventist believers united by a common passion to share the everlasting Gospel of Jesus Christ. We are committed to serving God through literature evangelism, public evangelism, Bible study, prayer, and Christian service, with the desire of preparing people for the soon return of our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ.

Our ministry began in September 2025 with fewer than ten members who shared the same burden of reaching people with the love of Christ. By God's grace, the ministry has continued to grow as more believers have joined our mission, bringing new ideas, gifts, and a shared commitment to spreading the Gospel.

we established a leadership structure to support our work effectively. The ministry is guided by dedicated leaders serving as the Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer, and Spiritual Leaders, who work together to coordinate activities and encourage every member to participate in the ministry.

Communication and fellowship are central to our ministry. We stay connected through our WhatsApp community, where members receive ministry updates, share ideas, and encourage one another. We also hold weekly Zoom meetings and WhatsApp calls to pray together, discuss ministry matters, plan future outreach programs, and review completed missions.

Brethren Mission Ministry is built on willing service and cheerful giving. Every member is encouraged to contribute according to his or her ability and to share mission ideas. Together, we prayerfully plan, carry out, and evaluate every outreach for the advancement of God's kingdom.

Since our beginning, God has blessed us with several successful evangelistic missions. Through every outreach, we have witnessed His guidance, protection, and provision. We firmly believe that by trusting in God, depending on the Holy Spirit, living a life of prayer, maintaining unity, encouraging participation, building strong teamwork, remaining committed, and giving willingly, our ministry will continue to fulfill its mission of sharing the everlasting Gospel.
