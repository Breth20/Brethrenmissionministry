About Brethren Mission Ministry

Welcome to Brethren Mission Ministry. We are a Christian ministry dedicated to sharing the everlasting Gospel through Literature Evangelism, Public Evangelism, Bible Study, Prayer, and Christian Service. Our desire is to lead people to Jesus Christ and prepare hearts for His soon return.

Who We Are

Brethren Mission Ministry is an independent Christian ministry of Seventh-day Adventist believers united by a common passion to share the everlasting Gospel of Jesus Christ. We are committed to serving God through literature evangelism, public evangelism, Bible study, prayer, and Christian service, with the desire of preparing people for the soon return of our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ. Our ministry began in September 2025 with fewer than ten members who shared the same burden of reaching people with the love of Christ. By God's grace, the ministry has continued to grow as more believers have joined our mission, bringing new ideas, gifts, and a shared commitment to spreading the Gospel. As the ministry expanded, we established a leadership structure to support our work effectively. The ministry is guided by dedicated leaders serving as the Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer, and Spiritual Leader, working together to coordinate activities and provide spiritual guidance while encouraging every member to participate in the ministry. Communication and fellowship are central to our ministry. We stay connected through our WhatsApp group, where members receive ministry updates, share ideas, and encourage one another. We also hold weekly Zoom meetings and, whenever necessary, WhatsApp calls to pray together, discuss ministry matters, plan future outreach programs, and review completed missions. Brethren Mission Ministry is built on willing service and cheerful giving. Every mission is supported through voluntary contributions from members, each giving according to their ability and willingness. We believe that every member has valuable ideas to contribute, and everyone is encouraged to propose mission opportunities. Together, we prayerfully discuss these ideas, plan our outreach, and evaluate each mission after it has been completed so that we can continue to grow and improve our service for God's kingdom. Since our beginning, God has blessed us with the opportunity to carry out several evangelistic missions. Through every outreach, we have witnessed His guidance, protection, and provision. These experiences have strengthened our faith and confirmed that God is leading this ministry according to His purpose. We firmly believe that successful ministry is built upon the guidance of the Holy Spirit, complete trust in God, earnest prayer, active participation by every member, Christian unity, commitment, teamwork, and willing donations. With Christ as our foundation, we remain dedicated to sharing His love and proclaiming the everlasting Gospel wherever He opens the door.

We believe that every believer has a role in fulfilling the Great Commission by sharing God's love with the world.
