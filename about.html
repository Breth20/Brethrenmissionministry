About Brethren Mission Ministry

Welcome to Brethren Mission Ministry. We are a Christian ministry dedicated to sharing the everlasting Gospel through Literature Evangelism, Public Evangelism, Bible Study, Prayer, and Christian Service. Our desire is to lead people to Jesus Christ and prepare hearts for His soon return.

Who We Are

Brethren Mission Ministry is a faith-based Christian ministry committed to spreading the Gospel of Jesus Christ to all people. Through the distribution of Christian literature, evangelistic outreach, discipleship, prayer, and community service, we seek to strengthen believers and reach those who have not yet heard the Good News.

We believe that every believer has a role in fulfilling the Great Commission by sharing God's love with the world.

Our Vision

To see every community transformed by the Gospel of Jesus Christ through faithful evangelism, Christian service, and discipleship.

Our Mission

To proclaim the everlasting Gospel through Literature Evangelism, Public Evangelism, Bible Study, Prayer Ministry, and Community Outreach while encouraging believers to live Christ-centered lives.

Statement of Faith

We believe:

  • The Holy Bible is the inspired Word of God.
  • Jesus Christ is the Son of God and the only Savior of humanity.
  • Salvation is by God's grace through faith in Jesus Christ.
  • The Holy Spirit empowers believers for Christian living and ministry.
  • Jesus Christ will return to establish His everlasting Kingdom.
