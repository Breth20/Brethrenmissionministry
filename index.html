Welcome to Brethren Mission Ministry

Brethren Mission Ministry is dedicated to proclaiming the everlasting gospel through literature evangelism, public preaching, digital evangelism, and Christian service. We believe that every person deserves the opportunity to know Jesus Christ and understand the truth found in God's Word.

Our Mission

To proclaim the everlasting gospel, distribute Christian literature, support evangelistic work, and prepare people for the soon return of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To reach people everywhere with the message of salvation through literature evangelism, public preaching, digital outreach, and Christian service.

  • 🤝 Community Care
  • 📢 Public Evangelism
  • 💻 Digital Evangelism
  • 🙏 Lay Evangelism
