Support the Mission

Brethren Mission Ministry is supported entirely through voluntary gifts from individuals who believe in sharing the everlasting Gospel. Your generous contribution helps us distribute Christian literature, organize evangelistic outreach and support community service. Every donation, regardless of its size, helps us reach more people with the love of Jesus Christ.

Ways to Donate

Brethren Mission Ministry currently receives donations through our Treasurer's official mobile money accounts. Local and international supporters may use the following payment methods where international M-Pesa transfers are available. In the future, we plan to introduce a registered LIPA Number and an official ministry bank account.

Why Your Donation Matters

Your generous support enables us to continue fulfilling God's mission by reaching more communities with the everlasting Gospel.

  • 📖 Distributing Christian literatures
  • 📢 Supporting public evangelistic meetings and lay evangelism
  • ❤️ Community outreach and helping those in need
  • 🙏 Supporting future mission projects

Our Commitment

Brethren Mission Ministry is committed to managing every donation with honesty, transparency, and faithful stewardship. Every contribution is prayerfully used to advance God's work and bless the communities we serve.
