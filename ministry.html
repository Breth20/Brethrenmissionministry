Our Ministry

Brethren Mission Ministry exists to share the everlasting gospel of Jesus Christ through literature evangelism, public preaching, digital outreach, and Christian service. We are committed to leading people to a deeper knowledge of God's Word and preparing them for the soon return of Jesus Christ.

📖 Literature Ministry

We distribute Christian books that teach biblical truth and encourage spiritual growth. Our mission is to place inspiring literature into the hands of people everywhere, helping them discover the love, hope, and saving truth of Jesus Christ.

❤️ Community Care

We seek to demonstrate Christ's love by supporting people in need through acts of kindness and encouragement.

🙏 Lay Evangelism

We support lay members to participate in evangelism.

💻 Digital Evangelism

We use Social media platforms to spread the message of salvation to people around the world.

📢 Public Evangelism

We organize and support public evangelistic outreach programs that proclaim the everlasting gospel and invite people to follow Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

To proclaim the everlasting gospel, distribute Christian literature, support evangelistic work, and prepare people for the soon return of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To reach people everywhere with the message of salvation through literature evangelism, public preaching, digital platforms, and Christian service.
