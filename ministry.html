Our Ministry

Brethren Mission Ministry exists to share the everlasting gospel of Jesus Christ through literature evangelism, public preaching, digital outreach, and Christian service. We are committed to leading people to a deeper knowledge of God's Word and preparing them for the soon return of Jesus Christ.

📖 Literature Ministry

We distribute Christian books, magazines, and tracts that teach biblical truth and encourage spiritual growth. Our goal is to place inspiring literature into the hands of people everywhere.

❤️ Community Care

We seek to demonstrate Christ's love by supporting people in need through acts of kindness, encouragement, and practical assistance whenever possible.

🙏 Lay Evangelism

We support and encourage lay members to participate in evangelism by sharing the gospel in their homes, communities, workplaces, and wherever God opens doors.

💻 Digital Evangelism

We use websites, social media, and other digital platforms to spread the message of salvation to people around the world.

📢 Public Evangelism

We organize and support public evangelistic meetings, Bible seminars, and outreach programs that proclaim the everlasting gospel and invite people to follow Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

To proclaim the everlasting gospel, distribute Christian literature, support evangelistic work, and prepare people for the soon return of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To reach people everywhere with the message of salvation through literature evangelism, public preaching, digital outreach, and Christian service.
